Getting there

Stoos is car-free. You leave the car at the valley station and ride up by funicular from Schwyz or by cable car from Morschach. The hotel's website has directions, parking and timetables.

Bring the kids

A hotel made for families

Stoos Lodge is a family hotel, so the kids will have as much fun as we do. While the grown-ups enjoy dinner, the play area is right next to the restaurant.

  • Indoor fun park

    200 m² of play space with a climbing wall, a trampoline and a ball pit.

  • The big slide

    An eight-metre slide goes straight from the restaurant down into the play area.

  • Kids' club

    An accredited mountain daycare with indoor and outdoor activities, run by the hotel's own team.

  • Family rooms

    16 family rooms with connecting doors, so everyone has space.

  • For the little ones

    Babies up to 2 stay free with a cot, and breakfast is free for kids up to 6.

  • Car-free village

    No traffic in Stoos, and snow right outside the door in December.

Details are from the hotel's family page. Please check prices and kids' club times when you book.
